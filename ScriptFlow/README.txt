ScriptFlow — Teleprompter for First Health Enrollment
=====================================================

INSTALLATION
1. Drag "ScriptFlow" to your Applications folder.
2. Open Applications, right-click ScriptFlow, and choose "Open".
   (First time only — macOS requires this for apps outside the App Store.)
3. If you see "ScriptFlow cannot be opened because the developer cannot
   be verified", go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down,
   and click "Open Anyway" next to the ScriptFlow message.

PERMISSIONS
ScriptFlow will ask for two permissions on first launch:
- Microphone: Required to hear your voice and track which word you're reading.
- Speech Recognition: Used on-device only — no audio leaves your computer.

Grant both for the teleprompter to work. You can manage these later in
System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone / Speech Recognition.

GETTING STARTED
- The ACA script loads automatically on launch.
- Click Play to start the teleprompter overlay.
- Read naturally — the script highlights words as you speak.
- Use the phase bar to jump between sections.
